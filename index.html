01 A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity is
the starting point.

I’m currently pursuing a Diploma in Computer Hardware Engineering at Government Polytechnic College, Palakkad (2025–2027).

My interests sit between the physical and digital sides of computing. One day that might mean configuring a server or bringing a network back to life; the next, writing code for a web app or testing an ESP32 on a workbench.

I learn by building, breaking, debugging and trying again. I care more about understanding why something works than simply getting it to run.
